# BearStudio

Two AI editors that work on your own PC. Bruno edits your talking videos into Reels; Bella cuts full podcast episodes into shorts that each tell a complete story.

- Website: https://pkrnganesh.github.io/bearstudio/
- Download: see [Releases](https://github.com/pkrnganesh/bearstudio/releases)

Free for Windows 10 and 11. No account, no upload, no watermark.
